    About me

    The developer behind the mask.

    My name is Hassan Jannan! I’m a junior at the University of New Haven studying computer science with a concentration in Game Design and Development. I’m a Unity developer and designer, and I work as a technical producer and team lead.

    I founded Voyager Signal Games, where I lead a 10-member team. Over the past two years, I’ve led four collaborative game projects. What I enjoy most is taking a gameplay idea and turning it into something people can actually play.

    My projects have taken me through dialogue, character controls, inventories, UI, AI behavior, and other gameplay mechanics. I like figuring out how those pieces fit together, building mechanics that are fun to use, testing what feels right, and learning enough to improve the code myself.

    As game development changes, I use AI to fill gaps in my knowledge and connect different parts of my workflow. Understanding why a solution works matters to me as much as getting it working.

    Persona 5 Royal is my favorite game, and it inspired me to start making games. Its bold design, story, and gameplay still influence what I build and how I want my work to feel.
