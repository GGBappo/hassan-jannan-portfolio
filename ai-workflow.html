AI Workflow

Learn the system.Connect the tools.

I use AI to fill gaps in my knowledge, understand unfamiliar code, and automate repeatable work. My approach is to give each tool a clear purpose and connect planning, implementation, and learning through a central orchestration layer.

Learning comes first

I use AI explanations to understand the code, add what I learn to my skill set, and develop the knowledge to debug problems when they arise.

Why this workflow?

More capability. More understanding.

For me, the value of AI comes from orchestration, learning, and faster iteration. Connecting these tools to my development workflow helps me reduce repetitive boilerplate, explore different approaches to a system, and spend more time understanding how the pieces fit together.

That makes AI a technical multiplier: I can take on more complex work while building the knowledge and skills to maintain it. The goal is to grow both what I can build and what I can understand, improve, and debug myself.
