AI Workflow

Clear direction.Reviewed results.

This portfolio is one example of how I collaborate with AI: I give specific feedback, use it to help implement changes, and review the result through repeated iteration.

In practice

From visual feedback to a working page.

01 / Define the change

Start with specific feedback.

I use screenshots and targeted comments to explain what needs to change, from card alignment and heading spacing to how buttons communicate that they are clickable.

02 / Add context

Keep the work grounded.

I point AI toward the current website and GitHub repository, and provide corrections about my roles, dates, and project contributions so the portfolio reflects my actual experience.

03 / Implement

Turn requirements into changes.

AI helps translate my feedback into changes to the site's HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, including project card layouts, navigation, accessible controls, and email links.

04 / Review and refine

Keep improving the result.

I review the published site and give another round of feedback. That includes removing repeated content, clarifying skills for hiring, and correcting language that overstates what I did.
